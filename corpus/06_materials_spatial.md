# Materials, Spatial Audio & Environmental Specifications

## 1. Advanced Acoustic Materials
- **Ear Cup Outer Shell**: 7075-T6 Aerospace Aluminum alloy, 5-axis CNC-milled and micro-bead blasted with hard-anodized ceramic coating to resist scratching and resonant vibration.
- **Ear Cushions**: Bio-based slow-recovery memory foam wrapped in breathable Japanese synthetic protein leather, minimizing heat buildup during multi-hour critical listening sessions.
- **Headband Core**: Cold-rolled 301 austenitic spring steel with carbon-fiber reinforced polyetherimide tension bands.

## 2. 9-Axis Gyroscopic IMU & Dynamic Spatial Tracking
- Sensor Suite: 9-axis motion sensor (3-axis gyroscope, 3-axis accelerometer, 3-axis magnetometer) running at 1000 Hz polling rate.
- Spatial Binaural Rendering: Computes head orientation in under 4ms latency, anchoring virtual soundstages in 3D Dolby Atmos, Sony 360 Reality Audio, and custom Ambisonics formats.
- Target Exploded Frame: 035

## 3. Environmental Ratings & Durability
- Ingress Protection: IP54 certified (splash, sweat, and dust resistant for outdoor mobile studio use).
- Operating Temperature: -15°C to 50°C (5°F to 122°F).
- Acoustic Drop Resistance: Tested to withstand 1.5-meter vertical drops onto hard concrete without driver voice-coil misalignment.
