# NovaPulse X1 Flagship Audiophile Headphones: System Overview

## 1. Acoustic Philosophy & Architecture
The NovaPulse X1 is an open-back hybrid reference headphone engineered for spatial audio precision, ultra-low harmonic distortion, and neural active noise mitigation. Designed with an acoustic chamber crafted from 7075-T6 aerospace aluminum, the chassis suppresses internal standing waves while maintaining an ultra-lightweight structural profile of 285 grams.

## 2. Exploded Visual Sequence Reference
The hardware assembly follows a precision modular mechanical layout corresponding to the animated teardown stages:
- **Phase I (Frames 001 - 045)**: Fully assembled headphone in acoustic test orientation. CNC-milled aluminum ear cups with micro-perforated acoustic release vents.
- **Phase II (Frames 046 - 090)**: Disengagement of magnetic memory-foam ear cushions and gimbal pivot assembly.

## 3. Wireless & Lossless Transmission
- Protocol: Bluetooth 5.4 LE Ultra-Wideband & 2.4GHz Low-Latency Auracast.
- Supported Codecs: LDAC (990 kbps / 24-bit 96 kHz), LHDC 5.0, aptX Adaptive Lossless, AAC, SBC.
- Wired Bypass: Direct 3.5mm analog bypass with pure OFC (Oxygen-Free Copper) silver-plated cable and USB-C 32-bit/384kHz DAC mode.
