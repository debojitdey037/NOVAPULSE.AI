# Transducer Engineering: 50mm Beryllium Drivers & Magnetics

## 1. Diaphragm Composition & Dynamics
At the core of the NovaPulse X1 lies a proprietary 50mm composite transducer featuring:
- **Substrate**: Ultra-thin 18-micron Polyether Ether Ketone (PEEK) base with exceptional damping factor.
- **Deposition**: Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) pure Beryllium crystalline layer (2.4 microns) applied to the dome.
- **Surround**: High-compliance silicone elastomer suspension allowing peak-to-peak excursion of up to 4.2mm without breakup distortion.
- Target Exploded Frame: 115

## 2. Magnetic Circuit: N52 Neodymium Array
- The motor system employs a custom dual-annular N52 Neodymium-Iron-Boron (NdFeB) magnet generating a gap flux density of 1.55 Tesla.
- Voice Coil: 4-layer copper-clad aluminum wire (CCAW) wound on a Kapton former, delivering a nominal impedance of 32 Ohms at 1 kHz and 108 dB/mW sensitivity.

## 3. Acoustic Frequency Characteristics
- Frequency Range: 4 Hz to 48,000 Hz.
- Total Harmonic Distortion (THD): Less than 0.02% @ 1 kHz (94 dB SPL), and under 0.15% across the entire 20 Hz – 20 kHz audible spectrum.
- Tuning Target: Modified Harman 2024 Target Curve with +2.5 dB sub-bass shelf and linear midrange coherence.
