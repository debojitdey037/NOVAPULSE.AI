# Modular Teardown & Disassembly Engineering Guide

## 1. Frame-by-Frame Disassembly Progression
The exploded view animation illustrates the modular servicing workflow engineered for Right-to-Repair compliance (10/10 repairability index):

1. **Frames 001 - 050: Initial Inspection & Yoke Detachment**
   - The CNC outer shell is secured to the precision steel gimbal pivot.
   - Pinned spring detents allow smooth 110-degree rotation.

2. **Frames 051 - 090: Cushion Removal & Magnetic Ring**
   - The memory-foam ear cushions utilize an 8-point neodymium magnetic latch with acoustic silicone sealing gasketing.
   - Simply twist and pull gently; zero glued adhesive is utilized.
   - Target Exploded Frame: 075

3. **Frames 091 - 135: Acoustic Baffle & Transducer Release**
   - Four Torx T3 precision screws secure the outer damping ring.
   - Lifting the acoustic baffle exposes the 50mm Beryllium driver assembly and protective stainless steel mesh.
   - Target Exploded Frame: 115

4. **Frames 136 - 180: Logic Board & Sensor Array**
   - The main NovaFlow PCB features gold-plated zero-insertion-force (ZIF) ribbon cable connectors.
   - Decouple the tactile capacitive volume sensor and ANC feedforward MEMS ribbon.
   - Target Exploded Frame: 155

5. **Frames 181 - 240: Battery Extraction & Structural Core**
   - The modular 600 mAh Li-Po pouch is held by a tool-less pull-latch mechanism (no toxic solvent required).
   - The stainless spring-steel headband core is exposed alongside carbon-fiber reinforcement struts.
   - Target Exploded Frame: 210
